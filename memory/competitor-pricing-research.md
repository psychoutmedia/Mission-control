# AI Agent Platform Research

> Competitor pricing research - completed Feb 20, 2026

## Pricing Models in 2026

### No-Code/Low-Code Platforms
- **Zapier/Make**: $5K-$15K to build
- **Gumloop/n8n**: Free tier, paid from $38/mo
- **Relay.app**: From $38/mo

### Custom Development
- **Simple agents**: $5K-$15K
- **Mid-complexity**: $15K-$75K  
- **Enterprise/Complex**: $20K-$60K to build
- **Full custom**: $500K+

### Usage-Based Pricing
- **Intercom Fin AI**: $0.99 per resolved conversation
- **Per-seat pricing**: Common in enterprise tools
- **API credits**: Many providers charge per call

## Key Players
1. **Intercom** - Fin AI agent, usage-based pricing
2. **Gumloop** - Visual AI workflows
3. **n8n** - Open-source automation
4. **Relay.app** - Simple AI agents
5. **Custom builds** - For enterprise with specific needs

## Market Insights
- AI agent market exploding (see $2.53T projection)
