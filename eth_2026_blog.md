# Ethereum in 2026: Consolidation After the Breakout

*From all-time highs to steady ground—ETH is catching its breath*

---

Ethereum had a year to remember in 2025. In August, ETH shattered its all-time high, reaching nearly $5,000—driven by a perfect storm of record ETF inflows, institutional adoption, and the SEC declaring ETH not a security. Staking also reduced liquid supply, creating upward pressure.

Now? ETH has pulled back to the $1,900–$2,500 range. That's a big correction from the highs—but it's not all bad news.

**Why the dip?** After any ATH breakout, consolidation is healthy. Markets rotated out of alts. Technical resistance at the 50-day MA has held. But the 200-day MA has been rising since August 2025, providing solid support around $1,900.

**What's ahead in 2026?** Two major upgrades are coming. **Glamsterdam** arrives in H1 2026, promising improved throughput and efficiency. **Hegota** follows later in the year with further gains. These upgrades could reignite bullish momentum.

**The big picture:** ETH remains the backbone of DeFi and smart contracts. Institutional demand via ETFs hasn't vanished—it's paused. With regulatory clarity now in place and network upgrades on the horizon, the foundations are strong.

Support at $1,900 is holding. If the upgrades deliver, $3,000–$5,000 could be back in play.
