Our Story

Lock Coffee was born from a simple obsession: the pursuit of the perfect cup. Founded in 2021, we set out to create a space where exceptional coffee meets timeless design.

We source our beans from micro-lot farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, roasting each batch in small batches to bring out unique flavor profiles. Our baristas are trained in the art of extraction, ensuring every shot is dialed to perfection.

More than coffee — we're about the moments. Morning quiet, afternoon catch-ups, evening reflections. Lock Coffee is your space to pause.
