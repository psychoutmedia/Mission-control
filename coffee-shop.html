Our Story

Founded in 2018, Bean & Brew began as a small neighborhood coffee cart with a simple mission: serve exceptional coffee while creating a space where community thrives.

Today, we source our beans directly from sustainable farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, roasting each batch in-house to ensure peak freshness and flavor.

Every cup we serve is made with love by our trained baristas who are passionate about the craft of coffee.
